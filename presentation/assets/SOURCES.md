The source deck introduces these routes under HONR-AV (Healthcare & On-Demand Rapid Autonomous Vehicles). Scheduled demo dates and proposed service capabilities are not presented as completed results. The recording visually shows cameras and LiDAR; its collection site, control mode and relationship to the hospital routes are not established by the slide.
