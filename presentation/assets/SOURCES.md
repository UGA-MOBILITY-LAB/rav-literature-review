## Previously inspected material not used

The rural drone communications recording in kickoff slide 27 and its poster are excluded. Drone-to-base-station footage does not substantiate the review's ground-vehicle networking finding or establish road-vehicle coverage, network performance or passenger-service outcomes. Neither the footage nor its web excerpt is part of the presentation. The connectivity finding uses the cited literature synthesis; a conceptual connection-loss demonstration is also excluded.

The Intro deck slide 10 camera/LiDAR recording and the kickoff slide 27 vehicle-avoidance video depict campus demonstrations. They are not used as rural imagery. Detailed route maps from Intro slides 7–8 and kickoff slides 28–30 are excluded at the user's request. Original decks and extracted media remain local. UW/Walworth monthly update materials were inspected but not published.

External pilot photographs remain separately credited in `../media.js` and appear only in the pilots chapter.
